Tools
=====

Everything in the Modulon toolchain is built from the repository.

+-------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| Tool                                | Purpose                                  |
+=====================================+==========================================+
| `Compiler <tools-compiler.rst>`__   | Compiles ``.mlon`` programs, AOT or JIT. |
+-------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| `Makefile <tools-make.rst>`__       | Builds the compiler from source.         |
+-------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| `Assembler <tools-assembler.rst>`__ | Built-in assembler support.              |
+-------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| `lines.sh <tools-lines.rst>`__      | Counts lines of source in the tree.      |
+-------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
